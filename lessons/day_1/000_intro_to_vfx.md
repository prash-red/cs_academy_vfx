# Day 1 — Introduction to VFX

This is the first lesson of the workshop and introduces students to the world of visual effects, CGI, and the idea of making the impossible feel believable on screen.

## Lesson goal

By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:

- describe what visual effects (VFX) and computer-generated imagery (CGI) are;
- recognize how movies combine live action with digital elements;
